ABOUT

Common Knowledge Base
Association

CKBA is a Swiss Verein—a membership-based, non-profit entity governed by its contributors. It reflects the natural progression of the CKB ecosystem.

It brings together the same people and efforts that have shaped CKB from the start under a more aligned and effective structure. It's not a reinvention, it's an evolution. A metamorphosis of the coordination layer into a more capable, coordinated, and participatory form.
